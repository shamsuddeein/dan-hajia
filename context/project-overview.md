# Dan Hajiya Investment — Company Profile & Operations Website

## Overview

A one-page corporate website for Dan Hajiya Investment (DAN HAJIYA INVESTMENT 19 LTD),
a Nigerian trading house operating from Kaduna. The website is structured as a
**professional company profile with an introduction to the owner and evidence of the
company's work**. It establishes who the company is, what it does, the people behind it,
and provides direct channels to contact the principal.

Rather than operating as an anonymous e-commerce shop with mock prices or automated
checkouts, all commercial activities are conducted through physical inspection, documented
provenance, and direct principal-to-principal communication.

## Approved Page Sequence

1. **Introduction (Hero)**: Identity, Kaduna headquarters, commercial trading proposition, and direct contact options.
2. **Company & Owner (About)**: Background, confirmed leadership (Director Umar Faruk Abubakar), CAC registration status, and physical premises.
3. **What We Do (Activities)**: Four core business divisions (Automotive Importation, Land Parcels, Residential Homes, Telecommunications).
4. **Selected Work**: Documentary evidence of work (vehicle container loading, cargo vessel staging, warehouse walkarounds, motorcycle demonstrations).
5. **Complete Gallery**: All 32 supplied media assets (12 photos and 20 videos) across 6 categories, including archived promotional graphics with clear disclaimers.
6. **Process & FAQ**: Transparent 5-step commercial dealing flow and answers to buyer due diligence questions.
7. **Company Details & Contact**: Kaduna registered office, confirmed public phone, WhatsApp, email, Post Code (`800283`), and Plus Code (`HCHR+M9F`).
8. **Footer**: Navigation repeat, registered entity name, and disclaimer regarding private invoice handling.

## Scope

### In Scope
- Single-page responsive web application built with React, Vite, and Tailwind CSS.
- Mobile-first responsive layouts across 320px, 390px, 768px, 1024px, 1280px, and 1440px.
- All 32 supplied media records preserved and accessible in the Media Gallery.
- Poster-first, click-to-play video playback with zero initial MP4 preloads.
- Accessible modal viewer with Tab focus containment, Escape dismissal, and native video control preservation.
- Confirmed corporate details derived directly from official CAC portal registration.

### Out of Scope
- Mock e-commerce carts, checkout, or sample prices.
- Publishing corporate bank account numbers (shared privately on invoices to prevent fraud).
- Unconfirmed personal biographies, founder claims, invented years in business, or fake testimonials.

## Success Criteria
1. Visitors immediately understand the business, its activities, its Kaduna base, and leadership.
2. Every visible claim is checkable against official CAC records or owner-confirmed facts.
3. All 32 unique manifest source IDs are accessible in the gallery.
4. Mobile navigation drawer remains accessible and full-viewport at the top and after scrolling.
5. Production build compiles cleanly with zero errors.
