# Feature 05 — Trust & Proof Section
Build the About section from `client-content.md` section D (about text) plus:
a photo row of the owner's real shop (outside + inside) with factual captions,
and fact cards — years in business, CAC registration (only if owner-supplied;
omit the card entirely if not), full shop address, goods categories. Every
fact renders from constants. Any fact still [OWNER TO SUPPLY] is omitted from
the UI (never shown as a guess) and stays listed in the tracker questions.
Done when a sceptical buyer finds checkable evidence here, and every visible
fact traces to a line in `client-content.md`.

Follow `context/media-requirements.md` when selecting images or clips. This
section is a curated, factual view; all 32 supplied files remain accessible
in the separate Media Gallery. Keep unknown business facts out of this
section, while allowing the underlying media into the gallery with neutral
visual descriptions. A clip in a `partners/` folder is not by itself evidence
of a partnership, country, completed deal, or date. Use it as verified partner
proof only once the owner confirms that context. Do not relabel vehicle-yard
or shipping footage as shop photographs. Selected clips use poster-first,
click-to-play behavior and share the gallery's single-active-player rule.
