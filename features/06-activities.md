# Feature 06 — Business Activities (What We Do)

Replaces mock e-commerce product cards with a professional overview of the company's
four core trading divisions:

1. **Automotive & Commercial Vehicles** (`#cars`): Foreign-used sedans, haulage vehicles, pre-purchase video walkarounds, and port clearance documentation.
2. **Land Parcels & Surveyed Plots** (`#lands`): Kaduna land plots, perimeter inspections, and title document review (C of O, official allocations).
3. **Residential Homes & Housing Units** (`#houses`): Duplexes and family homes, accompanied physical inspections, and deed handovers.
4. **Mobile Devices & Telecommunications** (`#phones`): Original smartphones, hardware condition verification, and wholesale supply.

## Implementation Requirements:
- Rendered in a clean 2x2 grid on desktop, single column on mobile.
- Link each activity only to media that actually supports it (Automotive links to vehicle footage in Gallery; Lands, Houses, and Electronics link to Kaduna office consultation without fake proof links).
- Direct WhatsApp enquiry CTAs with pre-filled topics.
